No Google US rank positions, overlap percentages, difficulty estimates or traffic numbers have been inferred from those failures.

## Evidence needed to finalize architecture

1. In Semrush Organic Research (now called Organic Rankings), choose the US database, one consistent date and device. For each relevant competitor, export the Pages report sorted by estimated organic traffic. Preserve URL, traffic estimate, traffic share and keyword count. Keep raw exports with their filters/date.
2. Open each leading relevant URL and export its URL-specific ranking keywords. Record query, US volume, position, traffic contribution, intent and exact ranking URL. Never substitute a domain-level keyword list. Deduplicate the same URL/query pairs and note overlapping volume estimates.
3. Group the observed queries by the task they seek to solve. Identify which clusters have meaningful demonstrated demand relative to this small site's scope. Avoid arbitrary volume thresholds and summed synonym forecasts.
4. For representative general, horizontal and rectangular queries, capture actual Google US organic results using the same location/device/date. Compare exact ranking URLs across queries and distinguish general tools from specialized ones. High overlap favors consolidation; consistent distinct specialized winners support a separate page. Record the actual result sets before making that judgment.
5. Inspect page-level link competition for the winning URLs using exact-URL backlink reports. Record available authority signals and inspect the strongest links manually. Do not substitute root-domain backlink totals.
6. Select the additional pages only when demand, intent separation, attainable competition and useful differentiated functionality jointly support them. Otherwise retain the provisional status or consolidate. Revisit decisions using Search Console query-to-page data after launch.
